CARE Bochum 26

Practical Information

Conference venue

Ruhr-Universität bochum
Universitätsstraße 150
44801 Bochum Süd

You can reach the campus via the U35 U-bahn line. Get off at the stop "Ruhr-Universtität". From the main train station (Hauptbahnhof) follow the direction "Querenburg"

The talks will take place in the building IC floor 03 room 604.
Note: Floor 03 and floor 3 are not the same floor.

Acomodation

For those who have asked, we have booked shared rooms in the Jugendgästehaus Bermuda3Eck. It is a walking distance away from the main train station and located very close to the Bochum city center.
Note: We are only offering to cover for participants' stay if they are staying in the acomodation we have picked.

Social activities

There will be a social dinner on thursday at 7pm at the Livingroom (Luisenstraße 9-13, 44787 Bochum).

For those who are staying until saturday morning, a social activity will be organised on friday afternoon. More on that later.

What to do in Bochum

There is more then enough to do in Bochum for a three day workshop. In case you need some here are our recomendations.

Food

  • If you want a currywurst, Brathwursthaus is the place to go.
  • You can go to Brauhaus Rietköter for generous german dishes
  • Nonno Mio offers arguably the best pizza in Bochum

Highlights

  • If you want to learn more about the mining history of germany and the Ruhr-area, you can visit the Deutsches Bergbau Museum
  • You can go watch Starlight express, a one of a kind roller skates musical about trains falling in love. Yes, you read that right.
  • Near the university campus you can visit the Botanical Garden.

Funding

We are able to provide funding for travel and accommodation for all the participants who asked for it. See above for the restrictions on the accomocation funding. Please keep in mind that since this workshop is meant to be a local event we do not fund plane travel. For people coming from far away we offer to fund an extra night to break the train trip into two days and make it more comfortable.
